# জলকণা (Jol Kona) — UX Strategy & Design System

## Brand Essence
"Every handmade product carries emotions."

## Target Audience
- Women 18-45 in West Bengal & Bangladesh
- Instagram-first shoppers
- Gift buyers seeking premium handmade items
- Festival & occasion shoppers

## Core UX Principles
1. Emotional First — every interaction evokes warmth
2. Minimal Distraction — let products breathe
3. Cultural Pride — Bengali identity woven subtly
4. Mobile Luxury — app-feel on phone screens

## Design System

### Colors
- Background: #FFF9F6 (Cream)
- Secondary BG: #FEF0E8 (Soft Pink)
- Accent: #C8956C (Rose Gold)
- Gold: #B8860B
- Text Primary: #2D2926 (Deep Charcoal)
- Text Secondary: #6B5E57
- Border: rgba(200,149,108,0.15)
- Glass: rgba(255,255,255,0.7)

### Typography
- Headings: 'Playfair Display' (serif, luxury)
- Bengali: 'Noto Serif Bengali'
- Body: 'Inter' (sans-serif, modern)
- Accent: 'Cormorant Garamond' (elegant serif)

### Spacing Scale
- xs: 4px | sm: 8px | md: 16px | lg: 24px | xl: 40px | 2xl: 64px | 3xl: 96px

### Border Radius
- sm: 8px | md: 16px | lg: 24px | xl: 32px | full: 9999px

### Shadows
- soft: 0 4px 24px rgba(45,41,38,0.06)
- medium: 0 8px 40px rgba(45,41,38,0.10)
- glow: 0 0 40px rgba(200,149,108,0.15)

### Animations
- ease-smooth: cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)
- ease-bounce: cubic-bezier(0.68, -0.55, 0.265, 1.55)
- duration-fast: 200ms | normal: 400ms | slow: 800ms
